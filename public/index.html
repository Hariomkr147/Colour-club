RULES & PAYOUTS

Know your game.

Outcome policy: Results depend on accepted bets. After predictions close, the server calculates the total payout for each possible number and chooses a result with the lowest total payout. Only equal lowest-payout results are selected randomly. This is not a draw based solely on luck or an independent random result.

  • 30-second, 1-minute, 3-minute and 5-minute rooms run independently.
  • Predictions lock for the final 5 seconds. Server time decides acceptance.
  • No Red with Green or Big with Small in the same room and round.
  • Up to 7 distinct numbers and 100 predictions per account per round.
  • Base stake: 1–1,000 points. Total maximum: 10,000 per prediction.
  • Welcome points are granted once per account. They cannot be purchased, transferred or redeemed.
  • Past results do not indicate what the next result will be.

ACCOUNT & PRIVACY

Your account information

We store your display name, email, password hash, session and prediction records. Administrators can review accounts and game activity and restrict access. Passwords and session tokens are not shown in the admin dashboard.

Session cookies support login and expire after seven days. Email is used for verification and recovery. There are no advertising trackers or third-party fonts.

Points have no monetary value. A public launch requires an operator contact, a defined retention policy and user data request handling. These operational details must be completed before inviting customers.
